# Methodology

The behavior classes were distilled from years of production support patterns in a conversational assistant used daily by non-technical staff. A class describes what people do, not who they are. Packs contain no real user records.

A persona is a parameterized behavior: voice, operators, patience, and a reading budget. It is not a prediction of how many of your users are like that. ChaosUsers therefore makes two kinds of claim only: a step will be among the top drop-off points, or a failure of a named kind will occur. It does not predict rates.

The judge never sees the persona's name, class, or the deterministic score. It must quote the transcript. Disagreement with the state checks is shown, and the grade stays with the checks.

After launch, `chaosusers verify` compares the hashed predictions with what you observed. If the receipt hash moved, the file was edited after the run.
